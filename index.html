MDAT Fitness

MDAT Fitness is a private fitness app for one person, for Windows and Android. It reads step, sleep, heart-rate and exercise data from the Google Health API, sets goals, gives rule-based suggestions, and turns consistency into quests and a small pixel-art gym.

What it does with your Google data

  • Reads your health data from Google, read-only. It never changes or deletes anything in Google Health.
  • Keeps your data on your own devices, plus one copy in the app's private folder on your own Google Drive so your phone and PC stay in step.
  • Sends nothing to the developer or anyone else. No ads, no analytics, no selling of data.
