MDAT Fitness

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

MDAT Fitness ("the app") is a personal fitness tracker for Windows and Android. This policy explains what data the app uses and what it does with it.

Data the app reads

With your permission, the app reads the following from the Google Health API, read-only:

  • Activity and fitness: daily steps and exercise sessions (type, duration, heart rate and calories).
  • Sleep: sleep sessions, time asleep and sleep stages.
  • Health metrics: daily resting heart rate.

It also stores what you enter yourself: goals, workouts you log, settings, and progress in the app's game.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show you your own charts, goals, suggestions, quests and summaries inside the app. All analysis happens on your own devices using fixed rules. No data is used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone.

Where the data is stored

  • On your own devices, in the app's private storage.
  • In the app's own hidden folder (appDataFolder) on your Google Drive, so the Windows and Android apps can stay in sync. Only this app can read that folder; it does not see any of your other Drive files.

The developer does not run any server and never receives your data.

Google sign-in

Sign-in happens on Google's own pages or your phone's Google account screen. The app never sees your password. On Windows, the sign-in token is stored encrypted by Windows on your PC.

Removing your data

Disconnect in the app's Settings and uninstall the app to remove local data. You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which also lets you delete the app's hidden Drive data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MDAT Fitness's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the developer's GitHub profile, github.com/EmEnGea.
